Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. :app uses a few that it cannot work without, and asks before using any other.

## Strictly necessary cookies

These keep you signed in, protect forms against forgery and remember the choices you make on screen. They need no consent and cannot be switched off.

| Cookie                                    | Purpose                                           | Lasts                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| session cookie                            | keeps you signed in during your visit             | 2 hours of inactivity |
| `XSRF-TOKEN`                              | protects forms against cross-site request forgery | 2 hours               |
| `remember_web_…`                          | keeps you signed in when you choose "Remember me" | until you sign out    |
| `appearance`, `theme`, `layout`, `locale` | the display and language choices you made         | up to 5 years         |
| `sidebar_state`                           | whether the side menu is open                     | 7 days                |
| `announcement_dismissed`                  | hides an announcement you closed                  | up to 5 years         |
| `consent`                                 | remembers your cookie choices below               | 1 year                |

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out of selling and sharing your personal information: marketing cookies stay off, whatever is chosen below.
