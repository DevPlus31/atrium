## Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

You have the right to **access** your data, to **correct** it, to have it **erased**, to **restrict** or **object to** its processing, to **data portability** (a copy in a machine-readable format) and to **withdraw consent** at any time without affecting earlier processing.

- **Download your data:** Settings → Privacy → Download your data (a JSON file).
- **Correct it:** Settings → Profile.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Profile → Delete account. This also erases your sessions, tokens and notifications; records we must keep by law are kept, as listed above.
- **Change cookie choices:** the "Your privacy choices" link at the bottom of the sign-in pages, or Settings → Privacy.

For anything else, write to :contact. We answer within one month. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

## Your rights (United States)

Depending on the state you live in (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and Texas), you have the right to:
